PROJECT NOTES / 06

Methodology & limitations

A local educational prototype for an auditable video-analysis workflow.

Problem and objectives

This project provides a browser-based workflow to validate video, manage analysis jobs and inspect traceable results stored in MongoDB.

Implemented analysis modes

Without optional weights, OpenCV validates decoding and records duration, resolution and frame rate. With configured Ultralytics COCO pose weights, the app applies an unvalidated fall-transition heuristic: prior upright torso evidence, a rapid body-center drop within 1.5 seconds, and horizontal torso posture persisting at least 0.6 seconds. A single low posture is insufficient.

Optional temporal fighting review

When a local X3D-M checkpoint is configured, overlapping four-second clips are classified as Violence or Non-Violence and candidates require two consecutive windows. This score is uncalibrated and requires human review. The model works at clip level, cannot identify a person or polygon zone, and may confuse sports or intense interactions with violence. The model source and setup are documented in docs/FIGHT_DETECTION.md.

Score and outputs

The rule score is 0.5 × min(1, drop fraction / 0.3) + 0.3 × min(1, torso horizontal ratio / 2) + 0.2 × min(1, horizontal persistence seconds / 1.2). It is an anomaly score, not prediction confidence. Annotated output depends on OpenCV codec support. Event times are relative to the source clip.

Face detection

OpenCV's bundled haarcascade_frontalface_default cascade independently locates frontal faces in sampled frames. It stores timestamps, per-frame counts, and boxes in MongoDB face_detection_frames. Counts are sampled observations, not unique people. This is face detection, not identity recognition; no identity or confidence score is produced. The cascade source, license notice, and limitations are documented in README. No model file is downloaded automatically.

Architecture

Browser → Flask routes → bounded in-process worker → OpenCV decoder → optional pose, temporal action model, and heuristics → MongoDB records and local media files.

Model and data

Pose weights are not bundled. Install the optional AI dependencies and configure POSE_MODEL_PATH as described in README. The app does not train models and includes no fall or violence dataset. This heuristic needs evaluation on consented, labeled videos before research claims can be made.

Ethics and privacy

The prototype does not identify people or assess dangerousness. Use consented footage, keep it local, and delete it after use. This demonstration has no login and requires additional authentication, isolation and operational security before public deployment.

Limitations and future scope

False positives and false negatives are expected; nearest-center pose association can switch people in crowded scenes. Fighting and general anomaly classification are not implemented. Future work should evaluate on documented data, improve multi-person tracking, and add durable jobs and access control.
